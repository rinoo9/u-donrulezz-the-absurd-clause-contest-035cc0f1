One original English sentence (166 characters), written as an absurd clause buried deep inside a terms-of-service agreement: deadpan legal boilerplate that quietly turns absurd — it reads like an ordinary intellectual-property assignment until it assigns ownership of the reader's shadow, and leases it out on weekends.

> By using this service, you agree that your shadow becomes the exclusive property of the Company and may be leased to third parties on weekends without further notice.

Run it: `python3 main.py`

The program is deterministic: it writes the same line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout on every run, with no input, no randomness, and no network access.
